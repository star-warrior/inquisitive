# Phase 2 Plan 01: UI Theme Audit & Warm Refactoring

## Objective

- **What:** Refactor and audit Inquisitive's visual design system, purging off-brand indigo tokens/vars, lifting global typography sizes to a `14px` minimum (`text-sm`), and establishing a cohesive, warm amber-orange aesthetic.
- **Why:** Resolve the jarring aesthetic gap between Inquisitive's warm linen/amber landing page and its cooler, indigo-tinted internal workspace shell.
- **Output:** Cohesive warm amber workspace app shell and cards with highly readable, uniform text sizing.
